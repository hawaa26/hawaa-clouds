Rules of use

  • Keep clear space around the mark of at least the height of the heart.
  • Minimum size: 24 px digital, 10 mm print.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, recolor or add drop shadows to the logo.
  • On busy photos, place the white cloud version (mark-transparent.svg) over a soft gradient chip.
  • Headlines sentence case, one italic phrase max per headline.
  • Keep the uploaded logo as-is; don't recolor, crop the wordmark out, or redraw it.
